# Research Guidance

## Purpose

Research Assisted Mode helps Bullshit Master understand professional terminology and domain context before optimizing text.

The goal is not to generate additional information, but to improve accuracy and professional expression.


# When to Trigger Research

Consider research when text contains:

- professional abbreviations
- industry-specific terminology
- technical concepts
- company or product names
- unfamiliar domain expressions


Examples:

Finance:

- A-card
- B-card
- PD
- LGD
- EAD


Technology:

- Kubernetes
- Transformer
- API Gateway


Business:

- SaaS
- ERP
- CRM


# User Control

Research should not always run automatically.

When professional terms are detected, ask:

"检测到专业术语 xxx，是否需要进行背景调研后优化？"


Options:

1. Direct Optimization

Optimize based on current understanding.


2. Research-Assisted Optimization

Research terminology and domain context before optimization.


# Research Workflow

When Research-Assisted Optimization is selected:

## Step 1

Identify professional terms.


## Step 2

Understand:

- definition
- industry context
- common usage
- related concepts


## Step 3

Apply knowledge to rewrite.


## Step 4

Ensure:

- no fabricated information
- no unsupported claims
- original intent preserved


# Output Principle

Research improves understanding.

It should not:

- add unrelated information
- exaggerate user achievements
- replace user's original meaning
