# 🧙 Bullshit Master

![MIT License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-blue)
![Codex Skill](https://img.shields.io/badge/Codex-Skill-purple)

> Transform ordinary words into professional expressions.

**Bullshit Master** is an AI writing optimization skill that transforms casual, vague, or task-oriented expressions into concise, professional, and value-oriented communication.

It preserves the original meaning while integrating professional facts, capability contribution, business impact, and evidence-based value elevation into one coherent paragraph.

---

# Philosophy

Bullshit Master does not create meaningless jargon.

It helps users:

- express real ideas more clearly
- highlight actual contributions
- communicate value professionally
- improve writing quality without changing facts

> Make your value visible. Don't make your words empty.

---

# Features

## ✨ Professional Writing Optimization

> Transform ordinary words into professional, valuable expressions.

Example:

**Before**

> 这个方案不太行。


**After**

> 当前方案仍存在进一步优化空间，建议结合实际需求及执行条件进行评估调整。

Value Enhancement focuses on:

- fact preservation
- business value
- project contribution
- capability building
- business impact
- evidence-based value elevation
- single-paragraph delivery

---

## 🔗 Four-Layer Value Framework

For business, finance, project, and performance scenarios, Bullshit Master follows:

```text
Professional Fact
    ↓
Capability Contribution
    ↓
Business Impact
    ↓
Value Elevation
```

Value Elevation summarizes the broader capability, management mechanism, or sustainable foundation supported by the facts. It does not invent strategic outcomes, measurable improvements, or industry-leading claims.

# Usage

Ask your AI assistant:

帮我优化下面这段文字：

Example:

**Input**

> 对齐A卡开发窗口和验证数据窗口，明确用5-6月数据进行双向测试，重新梳理上线打分映射逻辑。


**Output**

> 围绕A卡开发与上线验证要求，完成模型开发窗口与验证数据窗口的对齐，明确采用5—6月数据开展双向测试，并重新梳理上线评分映射逻辑，进一步完善从样本窗口定义、模型效果验证到线上评分应用的实施链路，提升模型开发与上线应用的一致性和可验证性，为A卡稳定落地及后续持续监测与迭代优化奠定基础。

Copy the skill folder into your Codex skills directory:


Restart Codex and start using:

```text
帮我优化这段文字
```
