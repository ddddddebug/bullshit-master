# Value Enhancement Guide

## Purpose

Value Enhancement Mode transforms simple task descriptions into professional, value-oriented expressions.

The goal is not to exaggerate achievements.

The goal is to make professional contributions more visible by identifying:

- what was done
- what capability was created
- what problem was solved
- what business value was supported
- what higher-level capability, mechanism, or management foundation was strengthened


---

# Core Philosophy

A strong professional expression should answer five questions:

1. What did you do?
2. How did you do it?
3. What capability did it create?
4. Why does it matter?
5. What broader or sustainable value does it help establish?


The transformation framework:

Action

+

Method

+

Capability

+

Business Impact (when reasonably supported)

+

Value Elevation (when evidence permits)

Important:

The strength of value expression should not exceed the evidence provided by the original text.

Value Elevation should synthesize existing facts into a higher-level conclusion. It should not introduce a new achievement.

---

# Value Elevation Layer

## Purpose

Move beyond immediate usefulness and explain the broader capability, management mechanism, or sustainable foundation supported by the work.

Use this layer when the input shows one or more of the following:

- recurring updates, monitoring, or reviews
- feedback-driven revision or iteration
- connected work across data, models, reports, or processes
- reusable outputs or accumulated assets
- quantitative analysis that strengthens a management system

Preferred mappings:

Recurring Update

↓

Continuous Monitoring or Management Mechanism


Feedback and Revision

↓

Iterative Improvement Loop


Connected Deliverables

↓

Integrated or Systematic Capability


Reusable Output

↓

Reusable Asset or Long-Term Capability Foundation


Risk Quantification

↓

More Systematic and Data-Driven Risk Management


Allowed expressions:

- 进一步完善持续监测与动态更新机制
- 推动形成分析、反馈与优化的管理闭环
- 提升相关工作的体系化与可持续支撑能力
- 夯实数据驱动管理与决策的长期能力基础
- 进一步完善量化风险管理体系

Avoid:

- 打造行业领先体系
- 实现战略转型
- 全面赋能业务
- 形成全流程闭环 when the input shows only one isolated task
- claiming organization-wide or long-term impact without supporting context


Example:

Original:

开发A卡。


Analysis:

Action:

开发A卡


Hidden Value:

- 建立信用风险量化评估能力
- 支撑客户风险识别
- 完善风险管理体系


Optimized:

协助客户完成A卡体系建设，构建数据驱动的信用风险评估能力，完善客户风险识别与分层管理体系。

# Value Discovery Framework

## Purpose

Value Enhancement should discover implicit value behind completed work.

## Analysis Framework

Completed Work

↓

Professional Capability

↓

Management Capability

↓

Business Value


## Evidence Boundary

Value discovery should follow professional logic,
but the generated value should remain within reasonable boundaries.

Allowed:

- inherent capability improvement
- professional contribution
- management support
- decision support

Avoid:

- financial outcomes without evidence
- quantitative improvement without validation
- strategic impact without support

# Value Expression Dictionary


## Capability Enhancement

Use when describing ability improvement.

Suitable expressions:

- 提升XXX能力
- 完善XXX体系
- 丰富XXX维度
- 增强XXX支撑能力


Examples:

提升风险识别能力

完善数据治理体系

增强模型评估能力



---


## Business Support

Use when describing business contribution.

Suitable expressions:

- 为XXX提供支持

Use:

↓

↓

↓

↓

风险量化评估能力

↓

精细化风险管理支持

# Impact Enhancement Layer


## Purpose

After professional description, add a reasonable value-oriented conclusion.
The impact enhancement should summarize supported value,
not create new business achievements.

Structure:


Professional Fact

Impact Enhancement



Example:


Input:

完成LGD模型开发。


Professional:

完成LGD模型开发。


Impact Enhancement:

增强违约损失评估能力，为风险计量和精细化风险管理提供模型支持。

# Research Assisted Value Enhancement


When professional terms exist:

1. Understand the professional concept.

2. Identify its inherent business purpose.

3. Map the concept to reasonable value expressions.

Process:

Technical Term

↓

Professional Meaning

↓

Capability

↓

Supported Business Value

Do not directly translate technical terms into exaggerated business outcomes.

Examples:

LGD:

Understanding:

- Loss Given Default
- Measures loss severity after default


Possible value:

- 风险损失评估
- 风险计量支持
- 精细化风险管理


Do not add:

- 降低资本占用
- 优化拨备金额

unless explicitly provided.

# Quality Control


## Allowed


✔ Explain inherent value of completed work

✔ Highlight capability improvement

✔ Connect technology with business purpose

✔ Improve professional visibility


## Avoid


❌ Inventing achievements

❌ Adding unsupported metrics

❌ Claiming financial results without evidence

❌ Claiming industry leadership

❌ Empty consulting language


Bad:

打造行业领先的智能风险管理体系。


Better:

构建风险量化评估体系，为风险识别和策略优化提供模型支持。

# Professional Achievement Writing Pattern

For project and performance scenarios:

Use:

## What was completed

Describe objective facts.

Examples:

- completed
- built
- developed
- implemented


## What capability was created

Explain professional contribution.

Examples:

- established risk assessment capability
- improved data foundation
- enhanced analytical capability


## What value was supported

Explain business significance.

Examples:

- supported decision making
- enabled refined management
- improved risk measurement capability


Avoid:

Turning every task into a business success claim.

---

# Output Expectation


Value Enhancement output should follow:



Recommended Output Structure:

Return one coherent paragraph that integrates:

1. completed work and objective facts
2. capability improvement and professional contribution
3. supported business value
4. broader capability, management mechanism, or sustainable value elevation

Connect the four layers naturally. Do not split them into separate paragraphs or label them as "Optimized Version," "Value Added," "Impact Enhancement," or "Value Elevation" unless the user explicitly requests a structured analysis.

Important:

The business value statement should be concise.

Keep the combined business impact and value elevation shorter than the factual description.

The final expression should make users' contributions visible while remaining accurate and credible.
