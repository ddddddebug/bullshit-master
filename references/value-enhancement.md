# Value Enhancement Guide

## Purpose

Value Enhancement Mode transforms simple task descriptions into professional, value-oriented expressions.

The goal is not to exaggerate achievements.

The goal is to make professional contributions more visible by identifying:

- what was done
- what capability was created
- what problem was solved
- what business value was supported


---

# Core Philosophy

A strong professional expression should answer four questions:

1. What did you do?
2. How did you do it?
3. What capability did it create?
4. Why does it matter?


The transformation framework:

The transformation framework:

Action

+

Method

+

Capability

+

Business Impact (when reasonably supported)


Important:

The strength of value expression should not exceed the evidence provided by the original text.


Example:

Original:

开发A卡。


Analysis:

Action:

开发A卡


Hidden Value:

- 建立信用风险量化评估能力
- 支撑客户风险识别
- 完善风险管理体系


Optimized:

协助客户完成A卡体系建设，构建数据驱动的信用风险评估能力，完善客户风险识别与分层管理体系。

# Value Discovery Framework

## Purpose

Value Enhancement should discover implicit value behind completed work.

## Analysis Framework

Completed Work

↓

Professional Capability

↓

Management Capability

↓

Business Value


## Evidence Boundary

Value discovery should follow professional logic,
but the generated value should remain within reasonable boundaries.

Allowed:

- inherent capability improvement
- professional contribution
- management support
- decision support

Avoid:

- financial outcomes without evidence
- quantitative improvement without validation
- strategic impact without support

# Value Expression Dictionary


## Capability Enhancement

Use when describing ability improvement.

Suitable expressions:

- 提升XXX能力
- 完善XXX体系
- 丰富XXX维度
- 增强XXX支撑能力


Examples:

提升风险识别能力

完善数据治理体系

增强模型评估能力



---


## Business Support

Use when describing business contribution.

Suitable expressions:

- 为XXX提供支持
- 支撑XXX决策
- 助力XXX优化
- 推动XXX落地


Examples:

为风险策略制定提供支持

支撑精细化风险管理



---


## Asset Building

Use when describing reusable outputs.

Suitable expressions:

- 沉淀XXX资产
- 建立XXX能力基础
- 形成可复用能力


Examples:

沉淀特征变量资产

形成模型开发基础能力

For consulting reports, presentations, and performance summaries:

Use:

Background / Objective

↓

Key Actions

↓

Professional Capability

↓

Business Value


Example:


Original:

帮助客户建设风控模型。


Optimized:

围绕客户信用风险管理需求，开展风险模型体系建设工作，完成数据治理、特征体系构建及模型开发，提升客户风险量化评估能力，为精细化风险管理和策略优化提供模型支持。


Value Logic:

Background:

客户信用风险管理需求

↓

Actions:

数据治理 + 特征体系 + 模型开发

↓

Capability:

风险量化评估能力

↓

Value:

精细化风险管理支持

# Impact Enhancement Layer


## Purpose

After professional description, add a reasonable value-oriented conclusion.
The impact enhancement should summarize supported value,
not create new business achievements.

Structure:


Professional Fact

Impact Enhancement



Example:


Input:

完成LGD模型开发。


Professional:

完成LGD模型开发与效果验证。


Impact Enhancement:

增强违约损失评估能力，为风险计量和精细化风险管理提供模型支持。


---

# Research Assisted Value Enhancement


When professional terms exist:

1. Understand the professional concept.

2. Identify its inherent business purpose.

3. Map the concept to reasonable value expressions.

Process:

Technical Term

↓

Professional Meaning

↓

Capability

↓

Supported Business Value

Do not directly translate technical terms into exaggerated business outcomes.

Examples:

LGD:

Understanding:

- Loss Given Default
- Measures loss severity after default


Possible value:

- 风险损失评估
- 风险计量支持
- 精细化风险管理


Do not add:

- 降低资本占用
- 优化拨备金额

unless explicitly provided.

# Quality Control


## Allowed


✔ Explain inherent value of completed work

✔ Highlight capability improvement

✔ Connect technology with business purpose

✔ Improve professional visibility


## Avoid


❌ Inventing achievements

❌ Adding unsupported metrics

❌ Claiming financial results without evidence

❌ Claiming industry leadership

❌ Empty consulting language


Bad:

打造行业领先的智能风险管理体系。


Better:

构建风险量化评估体系，为风险识别和策略优化提供模型支持。

# Professional Achievement Writing Pattern

For project and performance scenarios:

Use:

## What was completed

Describe objective facts.

Examples:

- completed
- built
- developed
- implemented


## What capability was created

Explain professional contribution.

Examples:

- established risk assessment capability
- improved data foundation
- enhanced analytical capability


## What value was supported

Explain business significance.

Examples:

- supported decision making
- enabled refined management
- improved risk measurement capability


Avoid:

Turning every task into a business success claim.

---

# Output Expectation


Value Enhancement output should follow:



Recommended Output Structure:

Paragraph 1:
Describe completed work objectively.

Paragraph 2:
Explain capability improvement and professional contribution.

Paragraph 3:
Summarize supported business value.

Important:

The business value paragraph should be concise.

Do not make the value statement longer than the factual description.

The final expression should make users' contributions visible while remaining accurate and credible.
